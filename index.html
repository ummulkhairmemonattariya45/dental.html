Common questions

How soon will you reply to my enquiry?

We reply the same working day, usually by phone, to confirm a time that suits you.

Does a checkup hurt?

No. A checkup is an exam and a gentle clean-up. If anything needs treatment, we explain it first.

How often should I get a checkup?

Most people benefit from one every six months. We'll recommend a schedule based on your own teeth.

Does sending an enquiry commit me to anything?

No. An enquiry is only a request for us to contact you, with no payment or obligation.
